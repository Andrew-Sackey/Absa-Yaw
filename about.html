Our Story

Titumma Agrochemicals was born out of the need to bring affordable and high-quality farming inputs to rural communities. We provide essential agrochemical products including pesticides, weedicides, and knapsack sprayers to empower local farmers.

Our team also trains farmers and extension officers to use our products safely and effectively, boosting yields and enhancing community well-being.

Who We Are

We are a dedicated team of agrochemical professionals and educators, committed to supporting sustainable farming practices. At Titumma, we believe in farming with purpose, educating farmers, and enabling the growth of thriving agricultural ecosystems in underserved areas.

Our expertise spans across crop protection, soil management, and sustainable agricultural practices that benefit both farmers and the environment.

Our Impact

Through our outreach and training programs, we have improved the productivity of hundreds of farmers and supported agricultural development in our communities.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, high-quality agrochemical products and comprehensive training to empower rural farmers, enhance agricultural productivity, and promote sustainable farming practices across underserved communities in Ghana.

Our Vision

To be the leading agrochemical partner in West Africa, recognized for transforming rural agriculture through innovative products, education, and sustainable practices that create thriving agricultural ecosystems.

Building Tomorrow's Agriculture

Our commitment extends beyond just selling products. We're building lasting relationships with farming communities, providing ongoing support, and continuously innovating to meet the evolving needs of modern agriculture.

Together, we're cultivating a future where every farmer has access to the tools, knowledge, and support they need to succeed.
